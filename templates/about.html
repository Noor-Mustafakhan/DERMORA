Our Origin & Philosophy

Dermatology designed around your skin, not generic templates.

For too long, aesthetic clinics in South Asia have imported western protocols uncalibrated for melanin-dense skin, or relied upon aggressive, unsafe chemical bleaches that temporarily mask concerns while causing long-term cellular trauma, barrier depletion, and rebound hyperpigmentation.

DERMORA was created to introduce a different standard: Modern Clinical Luxury with Editorial Beauty. We believe skin is complex living biology, not an artificial canvas to be aggressively stripped. Our protocols work in harmony with your natural acid mantle, respecting melanin depth and focusing on true cellular health, clear pores, and luminous vitality.

“We reject the pursuit of artificial, bleached ideals. True luxury in skincare is radiant, healthy skin that feels comfortable, resilient, and unmistakably yours.”
The DERMORA Standard

Our Clinical Approach

Five non-negotiable principles that govern every patient consultation, procedure, and follow-up.

01

Personalised Consultation

We dedicate unhurried clinical time to listen to your history, lifestyle, past reactions, and aesthetic goals.

02

Skin Phototype Assessment

Fitzpatrick phototyping and cross-polarized analysis determine exact melanin density and PIH risk before any device is selected.

03

Treatment Planning

Multi-session pathways structured with clear clinical milestones, avoiding aggressive shortcuts that compromise barrier integrity.

04

Patient Comfort & Safety

Sub-zero sapphire contact cooling, pharmaceutical-grade topical anesthetics when indicated, and soothing post-care compresses.

05

Professional Medical Ethics

Transparent expectations, zero false promises, and open medical counseling regarding which concerns require clinical physician referral.
