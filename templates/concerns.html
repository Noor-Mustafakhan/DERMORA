Dermatological Distinction: Cosmetic vs. Medical Indicators

A skin concern is not automatically a purely cosmetic condition. Skin darkening, sudden texture alterations, or persistent lesions may be cutaneous signs of systemic factors (such as insulin resistance in Acanthosis Nigricans, hormonal imbalances, or atopic diathesis). Persistent, irregular, or rapidly changing skin changes require assessment by a qualified healthcare professional.
